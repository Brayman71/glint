Privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026.

Glint is a desktop tool we run on our own computer to upload short videos to our own YouTube channels. This page explains what Google and YouTube data it uses.

What Glint accesses

  • Upload videos (YouTube scope youtube.upload): to upload the videos we choose to the connected channel.
  • Read basic channel details (YouTube scope youtube.readonly): to show which channel is connected and to read view counts of our own uploads.

Glint does not read your email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Where the data is kept

The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored only on the computer running Glint. They are not sent to any server of ours, and we do not run analytics or advertising. We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.

Use of YouTube API Services

Glint uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Glint's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Disconnect a channel in Glint, which deletes its stored tokens and revokes them with Google, or remove Glint at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask about or delete any data, email glintclipping@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
