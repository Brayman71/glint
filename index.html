Glint

Glint is a small desktop tool that turns stream clips into short vertical videos and uploads them to our own YouTube Shorts channels, such as Chat Lost It, Wanted Level and Joe Bart Clips.

It runs on our own computer. It picks clips that viewers already found worth clipping, frames them for vertical video, adds captions and a headline, and every upload is reviewed by a person before it goes public.

Google sign-in

Glint asks Google for permission to upload videos to a YouTube channel we choose and to read that channel's basic details. See the privacy policy for exactly what is used and how to remove access.
